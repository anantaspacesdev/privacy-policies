Ananta Japa — Privacy Policy

Simple, honest, devotion-first.

Effective date: July 4, 2026 Last updated: July 4, 2026 Developer: Aditya Upasani

🪷 Overview

Ananta Japa is a mantra counting application designed to support your daily spiritual practice. This policy explains clearly what data the app uses, where it stays, and what control you have over it.

Short version: Your japa data lives on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers — because we don't have any. The optional Google Drive backup copies your data to your own Google account, not ours.

📋 Data Stored on Your Device

All of the following is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to us:

  • Japa session history — mantra name, bead count, round count, session start/end timestamps, and your optional sankalpa (intention note).
  • App preferences — selected mantra, goal target, mala type, theme (dark/light), haptics, sound, reminder time.
  • Streak data — daily streak count and best streak, stored in device preferences.
  • In-progress session draft — temporarily saved so you don't lose a session if you switch apps.

☁️ Optional Google Drive Backup (Premium)

If you choose to enable cloud backup, the app signs you in with Google and writes your session history to your own Google Drive account — specifically the private appDataFolder, which is:

  • Invisible in your regular Google Drive file browser.
  • Accessible only by this app — no other apps or people can read it.
  • Stored under the minimal drive.appdata scope (the most restricted Drive permission).
We never see, touch, or store your Drive data. The backup goes directly from your device to your Google account.

Google's own privacy policy governs how Drive data is handled: policies.google.com/privacy.

💳 In-App Purchases

The Premium unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase was made (or not). We never see or store your payment details — Google handles all of that.

🔔 Notifications & Reminders

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is processed entirely on-device using Android's WorkManager — no network call is made and no data leaves your phone.

🔐 Android Permissions Explained

Permission Why it is needed
INTERNET Required for the optional Google Drive backup to reach Google's servers.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether a connection is available before attempting a backup.
VIBRATE Powers the haptic feedback on each bead count (can be turned off in settings).
BILLING Allows the app to communicate with Google Play for the Premium unlock.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends the daily japa reminder notification you schedule in settings.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedules your reminder after the device restarts so it isn't lost.

🔗 Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google services, each governed by Google's privacy policy:

  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchase processing.
  • Google Sign-In / Google Drive API — optional backup (Premium only).

The app does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, crash-reporting services, or any other third-party data-collection tools.

👶 Children's Privacy

Ananta Japa does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account registration and stores all data locally on the device.

🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion

  • All local data is deleted when you uninstall the app from your device.
  • If you used Google Drive backup, you can delete the backup file by disconnecting your Google account inside the app, or by removing the app's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • Purchase receipts are managed by Google Play and subject to Google's retention policy.

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
