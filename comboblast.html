2. What ComboBlast Does

ComboBlast is a classic grid-based block puzzle mobile game designed for brain training and relaxation. Core gameplay features include:

  • 8x8 jewel block puzzle board with drag-and-drop mechanics
  • Dynamic line-clearing, multi-line combos, and combo streak multipliers
  • Power-up mechanics (Rotate, Hold Pocket slot, Bomb blast)
  • Auto-generated offline Daily Missions and streak tracking
  • Local high-score tracking and player statistics (games played, highest combo, total lines cleared)
  • Multiple visual color themes and haptic vibration feedback

All core game algorithms, piece generation, collision detection, and daily missions execute 100% on your device without communicating with any external game server.

3. Data We Collect and Where It Is Stored

3a. Gameplay Data (Stored Locally On-Device Only)

When you play ComboBlast, the following data is saved strictly to your device's private SQLite database (via Android Room) and DataStore:

  • Player Statistics – high score, current score, total games played, total lines blasted, and max combo achieved.
  • Inventory & Economy – virtual coin balance, unlocked themes, and counts for power-ups (Rotates, Bombs, Holds).
  • Daily Missions – daily progress, completed tasks, and streak claim dates.
  • Preferences – sound effects volume, music toggle, haptic vibration toggle, and selected visual theme.

This data never leaves your device. We do not transmit or sync your game progress to any remote server or cloud database.

3b. Data Collected by Third-Party Services (When Online)

When your device is connected to the internet, certified third-party SDKs integrated into ComboBlast may collect standard pseudonymous technical data to deliver ads and process in-app transactions:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) used by Google AdMob for frequency capping, ad delivery, and fraud prevention.
  • Device & Network Information: IP address (for coarse geographic ad serving), device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Performance metrics to detect application crashes and improve stability.

6. Advertising and User Choices

ComboBlast is monetized through advertisements to keep the game completely free to play. You have full control over personalized ads:

  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • If you play completely offline (in Airplane mode or without Wi-Fi), no ad requests will be transmitted.
  • Rewarded video ads are strictly optional and only play when you explicitly choose to tap "Watch Ad" to earn extra power-ups or continue a game round.

7. In-App Purchases & Payments

Any optional in-app purchases are handled directly through the Google Play Store billing system. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all personal gameplay progress is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over data retention:

  • You can clear all app data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > ComboBlast > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling ComboBlast automatically and permanently deletes the local SQLite database, saved scores, coins, and preferences from your device.

9. Security

Because ComboBlast does not transmit or store gameplay records on external servers, the risk of cloud data breaches is eliminated. Your local game data is protected by the Android sandbox architecture, which isolates app storage from unauthorized access by other applications.
